## Strategy and Director decisions

The five-crate Rust workspace, Tokio and bundled rusqlite are retained. One supervisor owns a continuously reaped JoinSet. Nonwaiting admission bounds connections and requests before handler creation/body allocation; completed but unreaped entries remain counted. Rejections close inline without a task waiting for capacity. Absolute header/frame/write deadlines retain their original expiry through partial progress. A bounded channel feeds one dedicated, tracked SQLite worker; only doctor enters it, while hello/health bypass SQL.

Shutdown stops admission, cancels database work, aborts/reaps handlers, joins the actual native worker, closes SQLite and removes the owned socket under one 3,500 ms deadline. The instance lock remains held through joining. Bounded busy/progress/interrupt handling addresses executing SQL and start/cancel races. Listener resource-pressure recovery uses inline backoff capped at 100 ms, stays responsive to signals and reaping, and creates no retry tasks; fatal errors follow accounted cleanup. Optional `ARC_RUNTIME_REPORT` is a protected create-new file with a fixed 4,096-byte serializer; successful observed process exit and complete parsed accounting are required. A report file's existence alone is insufficient.

Technical Director advice accepted: reuse existing interfaces/security boundaries, account for retained tasks and canceled queued jobs, use one SQLite owner, bound busy waits and join the worker rather than detach it. Dependency changes stay limited to required Tokio sync/time, dev test-util, rusqlite hooks and the existing serde_json edge. A pool, ORM, extra runtime, platform migration and benchmark framework were unnecessary.

Design Director advice accepted: preserve the two-command CLI and fail-fast recovery guidance in [bounded IPC recovery](bounded-ipc-recovery.md) and [runtime usage](runtime-identity-usage.md). Automatic retries, new progress commands and participant testing are deferred; no user observation established their value for this repair. Predecode overload retains `DAEMON_UNAVAILABLE`; decoded DB overload uses additive protocol-1 `DB_BUSY`, mapped by the unchanged CLI fallback to exactly `IPC_ERROR`. Successful CLI text and exit behavior remain intact. Health independence assumes transport capacity; there are no reserved health slots during complete connection/request saturation.

Marketing advice accepted only as evidence discipline and scope control. [Value-scope assessment](bounded-ipc-value-scope.md) distinguishes inferred developer/operator needs from demand. Paid assessments, outreach, case-study distribution, registry work, sponsorship and retention experiments are deferred. Expected benefits are fewer hangs and more predictable recovery; customer time saved, support cost, conversion, churn, retention, revenue, margin and ROI were not measured. No commercial outcome is claimed.
